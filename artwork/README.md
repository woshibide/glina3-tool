# Cup artwork

Draw on a copy of `cup-template.png` and keep the full 1024 × 350 px canvas.
The top reaches toward the rim, the centre faces the viewer on load, and the left and right edges meet behind the cup.
Black raises the clay by 1 mm, white leaves it smooth, and grey gives intermediate relief.
Strokes about 9 px wide correspond to 2 mm on the cup.

Save finished PNGs beside the template and rebuild.
The preview picks one image per page load.
The blank template is excluded from the rotation.
